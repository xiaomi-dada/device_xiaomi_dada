## Firmware images for Xiaomi dada
##
## Intentionally empty.
##
## Firmware is handled entirely by vendor/xiaomi/dada-firmware, which both
## supplies the images (add-radio-file) and declares AB_OTA_PARTITIONS. Listing
## them here as well produced a second, generated AB_OTA_PARTITIONS block in
## vendor/xiaomi/dada/BoardConfigVendor.mk naming all 33 images, while the
## images themselves were never extracted into vendor/xiaomi/dada/radio/ -- so
## packaging demanded images that do not exist:
##
##     add_img_to_target_files.py, in CheckAbOtaImages
##     AssertionError: Failed to find abl.img
##
## That is also why vendor/xiaomi/dada/Android.mk had been hand-edited to say
## "Radio images are handled by vendor/xiaomi/dada-firmware".
##
## If firmware is ever moved back under this tree, note that extract_utils emits
## AB_OTA_PARTITIONS for *every* entry here as soon as any one carries ;AB.
